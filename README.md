# Defense Prime Contract Analysis

## Project Status
🚧 In progress — currently building out data collection and initial analysis.

## Business Question
How has federal contract spend with major U.S. defense primes (Lockheed Martin, 
RTX/Raytheon, and Northrop Grumman) shifted across military branches and 
contract types over the past several fiscal years, and what does that reveal 
about where each company's growth is concentrated?

## Data Source
Federal contract award data from [USAspending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov/), 
the official U.S. government spending transparency platform. Data is publicly 
available and free to use.

## Background
This project builds on prior quantitative work analyzing structural decline 
in the collision repair industry due to ADAS and autonomous vehicle technology, 
applying the same analytical approach to a new sector of interest.
